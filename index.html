🚀 RTL Fixer

Drag this button to Safari’s Favorites Bar

Mac · Safari

  1. Display the Favorites Bar with ⌘ + Shift + B.
  2. Drag 🚀 RTL Fixer above into the Favorites Bar.
  3. Open a webpage and click the bookmark to enable RTL Fixer. Click it again to turn it off.

iPhone / iPad · Safari

  1. Tap the Share button, then choose Add Bookmark.
  2. Save it as RTL Fixer, then open Bookmarks.
  3. Long-press 🚀 RTL Fixer, choose Copy Link, then edit the bookmark and paste it as the bookmark address.
  4. On any page, tap the bookmarks icon and select RTL Fixer. Tap it again to turn it off.
